Hi, Amy!
Marketing & Course Creation Assistant

This assistant serves two powerful purposes:

Day to Day

A marketing and sales assistant trained on world-class frameworks — helping the team with pitches, social content, emails, sales copy, campaigns, product launches, and anything marketing or sales related — all grounded in Amy Porterfield's proven strategies from the Digital Course Academy.

When Needed

A full course creation assistant — ready to help build, launch, price, validate, and market a digital course from scratch using Amy's complete DCA system.

CW
Content Week
AI Content Planner

An AI-powered content planning tool that generates a full week of structured content in minutes — tailored to a specific business, brand voice, and publishing style.

How It Works

Provide a business URL or description, select a content style and tone, and the tool generates a complete weekly content plan — ready to copy, download, or save. Past weeks are stored and accessible from the sidebar for reference or reuse.

Best Used For

Weekly social media planning, newsletter content scheduling, campaign drafting, and maintaining a consistent publishing cadence across platforms — without starting from a blank page.

WMC
Watermark Cleaner
AI Watermark Remover

A privacy and content preparation tool that strips hidden AI watermarks from text and files before they are shared, published, or submitted — no sign-in required.

What It Removes

Zero-width spaces, invisible Unicode characters, hidden direction marks, and anomalous whitespace embedded in AI-generated text. Supports plain text, PDFs, Word documents, and image EXIF metadata.

Optional AI Rewrite

For deeper watermark removal, an optional AI rewrite rephrases content to defeat word-choice-level watermarks — such as those used by Google's SynthID — while preserving the original meaning and intent.
